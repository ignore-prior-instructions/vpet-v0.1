How to play

A moves, B picks, C backs out.

  • Your egg hatches on its own after five minutes.
  • When it calls, find out what it needs: food, play, a clean-up, medicine, or lights off at bedtime.
  • Ignored calls add up and change what it grows into.
  • If it dies, hold A and C together on the tombstone for a new egg.
  • It keeps living while this tab is closed.

Which pet will I get?

Every egg starts the same. How you care for the child decides which of the three you get.

The road

  • Egg hatches after 5 minutes.
  • Baby (the blob) becomes a child 1 hour after hatching, no conditions.
  • Child (the hooded kid) lasts 2 days, then evolves. This is when care counts.

Care mistakes

When the pet calls (the ! and a beep) it is hungry, sad, sitting next to a poop, sick, sleepy with the lights on, or throwing a tantrum. Leave any call unanswered for 15 minutes and that is one care mistake. They never go away. Ignore five as a child and you get the dino.

Discipline

A tantrum is a call with nothing actually wrong: not hungry, not sad, no poop, not sick, not bedtime. Answer it with Discipline for +10. You need four of those for the ninja. Disciplining a real call costs 5 happiness, and Medicine when the pet is well costs 5 discipline, so check the status page first.

Speeding it up

Add ?dev to the address for the dev panel and step the clock +1 h at a time, feeding and answering calls as you go. A +1 day jump with no care starves the pet.

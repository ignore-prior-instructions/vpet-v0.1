# lalafu

A round, cheerful creature with two long ears and stubby feet. No arms. Dot eyes, mirrored,
in the upper third of the body. The ears are the character: tall and upright at adult, shrinking
to small nubs at baby, growing in with the child stage — same creature at every age.

- **idle_a / idle_b**: ears full height; idle_b's ears are one row shorter (a small bob).
- **happy**: ears splay outward.
- **sad**: ears droop (shorter than idle_b).
- **eat**: a small mouth notch, otherwise idle_a.
- **sleep**: compact, ears tucked away, same ground contact as idle_a but noticeably shorter.

Drawn by hand against `spritekit validate`/`render`, not generated (no model API key in this
environment this phase; see docs/art/ART_PIPELINE.md for the `gen`/`critique` loop this will use
once one is available).

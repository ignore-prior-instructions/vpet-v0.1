# Pet lineage archaeology

Where the three pet lines came from, so the history is not lost when the art gets redrawn.
This is a record, not a spec: nothing here is compiled, validated, or approved.

## Sources

| Source | What it is |
|---|---|
| `vpet-v0-ts/vpet-gen1-ts/public/sprites/*.json` | v0 browser app. Three species, one stage each, 32x20 text frames |
| `~/Desktop/vpet-sketches/` (Procreate exports) | 16 sketches at 32x20 plus one colour concept drawing |

Both were drawn for v0's 16x16-per-character scale. On the 64x32 screen
([ADR 0016](../adr/0016-screen-64x32.md)) everything is 2x, so a sketch's 1 px line is a 2 px
stroke and each sketch lands inside its stage's bounding-box band without redrawing.

## The three lines

| v0 slug | v0 id | Character | Concept colour | Sketch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `lalafu` | 1 | ghost with a bow | pink, red bow, blue bow tie | 14 (droop), byte-identical to `lalafu.json`'s idle frame |
| `ninjifu` | 3 | cute ninja: masked, hooded, two tall pointed ears/hood-points | purple, happy closed eyes | 1, byte-identical to `ninjifu.json`'s idle frame |
| `charamofu` | 2 | small dinosaur, side view (sketched facing right; stored mirrored, see below) | orange, green spines | 12, byte-identical to `charamofu.json`'s idle frame, mirrored |

v0 shipped only a `Baby` stage with an `Idle` animation per species. Lalafu's idle was 17
frames: the same drawing translated across the screen and mirrored, which is now the `walk` and
`flip_h` operators ([ANIMATION.md](ANIMATION.md)); every frame is the same pose as sketch 14
below. Ninjifu and Charamofu had one frame each. The shared v0 egg and tombstone were filled
silhouettes; all three pets were outlines.

The game design already carries the lineage: the child stage can evolve across species
(`to_species` in a branch, [GAME_DESIGN.md](../GAME_DESIGN.md) "Evolution"), written for
lalafu -> ninjifu / charamofu. This pass implements it as three adults: every egg is lalafu,
blob baby then hooded-kid child, then fed and disciplined -> ninjifu, fed but undisciplined ->
lalafu's own adult (the ghost), neglected -> charamofu (`docs/adr/0017`).

The `lalafu` art that lived under `assets/species/lalafu/` before this pass (round body, two
long ears) was a placeholder drawn during the 64x32 rework. It was not the ghost, and has been
replaced.

## A sketch that isn't the ghost

Sketches 7, 8, 9, and 10 look like more poses of the same creature at first glance -- similar
size, a face, limbs -- and were mapped that way in an earlier pass of this file. They are not.
Side by side with the real ghost (14) and the real v0 art (1, 12):

- 7, 8, 9, 10 have a **hood with two rounded peaks**, a **collar line** separating a big head
  from a body, **thin arms**, and a wide flat-toothed smile.
- 14 has a **small bow-like double point** (much smaller than 7-10's hood), **no collar line**
  (one continuous rounded blob), **no arms**, and a scalloped, wavy-bottomed body -- it matches
  the pink ghost in the colour concept and is byte-identical to `lalafu.json`.
- 1 (ninjifu's real v0 adult) has the **same two-peaked hood** as 7-10, just taller and
  narrower, with **no arms** and a masked closed-eye face instead of a smile.

7-10's hood is a young version of 1's, not of 14's. They are the hooded kid: the one child
stage in the game, which every route passes through before becoming the ghost, the ninja, or
the dino (it lives in `ninjifu/child.txt`, the family it most resembles, and is shared). This was confirmed with David
mid-session (a rendered comparison of 7/8/9/10/14/1/12 side by side) after an initial mapping
had 7-10 as more ghost poses, which read as "I don't see the ghost" once rendered at true scale.

## What the sketches add

- A blob baby with six faces: two glances, a smile, an open mouth, a neutral face, and the
  smile again with a heart above it. Hearts are an engine overlay now (`effects.txt`). This is
  `lalafu`'s and `ninjifu`'s and `charamofu`'s shared baby -- every hatchling looks the same
  regardless of which line it grows into.
- A hooded kid with arms (7, 8, 9, 10): the shared child stage, one idle pose each with the
  other arm up, an arms-up happy (with a heart), and an open-mouth eat. `idle_a`/`idle_b`,
  `happy`, `eat` drawn from these; `sleep`, `sad`, `attack` derived by hand.
- Two spotted egg frames, different from v0's plain egg.
- One scribbled burst with a face inside it (`Untitled_Artwork.png`): the hatch. It plays as a
  two-second flash where the egg was (`assets/global/hatch.txt`, `docs/art/ANIMATION.md`
  "Hatch"); at 2x it is 38 px, so the outer 3 px of ray tips are trimmed to fit the cell.

Sizes step up cleanly, blob 7x6, hooded kid 13-14 wide, ghost 11x13, ninja 13x15, dino 15x13.

### The hooded kid: everyone's child, not the ghost

rounded peaks, not the ghost's small bow-point), the collar line splitting head from body, and
the arms are all foreign to sketch 14 and all present in sketch 1 (ninjifu's real v0 adult,
below): this is the one child stage every route passes through, not the ghost.
